# Independent visual audit — ISEC master, 7 September 2026

This audit compares the five existing `renders/final` images (via their full contact sheet) against visually inspected Payette photographs. These are not camera-matched pairs; geometric corrections below are supported by visible form, while numerical sizes require plan/camera calibration. No Blender changes were made by this agent.

## Highest-impact corrections across the scene

1. Reconstruct the visible depth of the laboratory frontage. The model's glazing exposes one empty white corridor and reflections of repeated pink chairs. In the photographs, the facade reveals a write-up strip with gray rectangular columns, light oak desks, monitors, gray task chairs and linear suspended uplights; a second glass partition reveals white laboratory benches, shelving, lime walls and services. This complex layering is a defining feature and should be represented from observed references while keeping laboratory doors closed. The current emptiness makes every camera read as a generic mall.
2. Correct the lower staircase and its relationship to the concrete auditorium wall. The principal photograph shows an elongated lower ascent running diagonally from the foreground toward the spiral, separated from the broad stair on the other side of the large concrete body. The present compact curl, generic cylinder, and disconnected diagonal stair produce a different building. Coordinate calibration must precede surface details.
3. Replace flat/finely faceted stair undersides with a smooth sculpted soffit and a few real panel seams. Current radial wedge shading is highly visible in camera 04 and impossible in the references. Preserve planar treads; smooth the continuous white shell independently.
4. Restore ground classroom glazing/openings and the deep undercroft. The actual white band below the laboratory curtain wall has regularly spaced circular air outlets; below it, large glazed or open classroom bays reveal white tables and gray/teal chairs, separated by narrow light-wood door/stacking panels and round white columns. Current blank gray walls and isolated brown doors erase this spatial relationship. Doors can remain closed, with modeled visible frontage only.
5. Rebuild social hubs and office frontage. Real galleries alternate glass office fronts, curved deep-gray walls, rounded white kitchenette bars, gray base cabinets, vivid photographic murals and lime portal ends. Current identical opaque doors and uniform gallery strips are architecturally wrong.

## Camera/validation procedure

Crop letterboxing from references before determining aspect ratios. Camera matching must lock verticals (architectural photographs typically shift lens rather than tilt), then fit shared level lines, stair axis and facade curve. Mark 8–12 image landmarks and compare projected positions. Do not use broad visual similarity in a mismatched 18mm perspective to justify geometry. Avoid glass reflection duplicates creating false office fronts. Retain before/after camera pairs and record five discrepancies on every iteration.
