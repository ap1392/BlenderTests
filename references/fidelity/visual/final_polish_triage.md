## A — Must fix for the requested professional visualization

1. **Glazing/interior contrast.** REF04 and REF10 remain noticeably hazy and flat compared with the source, while REF01 has large nearly uniform blue spandrel fields. Preserve the documented vision/spandrel/frit construction, but test vision-pane roughness, tint/absorption, duplicate glass layers and reflection intensity separately. Use a fixed camera and exposure so a clearer result is not merely brighter. Keep physically plausible reflections; do not make every pane invisible. Success: service uprights, desks and circulation remain readable through vision panels while glazing retains reflected architecture.
2. **Material hierarchy and illumination.** The building currently reads as uniformly clean gray-white surfaces, bright blank exterior glazing and broad flat interior light. Establish one coherent daylight/fixture solution, with brighter daylight-facing areas, plausible shaded soffits, softer contact shadows and restrained warm interior fixtures. Calibrate against REF01, REF03 and REF05 together. Do not bake unrelated exposure corrections into every cinematic shot or use dramatic darkness to conceal geometry. Avoid specifying an undocumented exact sun time.
3. **Hero furniture silhouettes.** The tall magenta ground chairs are prominent and still look assembled from generic shells. Source photos clearly support their flared upper back, deep enveloping seat and narrow transition into the base. Correct the visible silhouette and joins of this one family before a ground-level hero approach. Keep exact manufacturer attribution unverified. The existing fine social-chair quilting should remain subtle; do not reintroduce inflated relief.
4. **Stable cinematic material response.** Test close glass/metal highlights, carpet, ceiling blades and fine grip strips in a short moving-camera preview before committing to the movie. Static Cycles images cannot establish temporal stability or Unreal parity. Thin highlights and repeated fine lines are likely stress cases, not presently proven defects.

No new large architectural break is demonstrated by the completed post60 set. Previously corrected landing, floor, terrace and fixture-clearance defects should not be relisted as current failures.

## B — Should fix with a short, evidence-based pass

- **Oak:** preserve directional veneer grain but replace overly regular striping with restrained irregular grain/pore variation. The Wood056 trial as rendered was flatter and less convincing than the current material; do not adopt it solely because it is scanned. A subtle blend or corrected directional material is safer than restarting all wood shading.
- **Concrete:** retain board direction and relief hierarchy, reduce any repeated conspicuous patches, and add only shallow pore/roughness variation at credible scale. The bitmap remains an AI reconstruction, not a site scan. Do not make the wall resemble deeply weathered masonry.
- **Terrazzo/white steel:** distinguish the fine aggregate tread/floor finish from smooth painted ribbon surfaces through microscopic texture and roughness, not coarse bump. Current uniformly white surfaces flatten the construction distinction visible in the photographs.
- **Metal and glass fixings:** remove overly sharp plastic-looking highlight transitions; use controlled bevels and subtle roughness. Keep existing radial glass normals and thickness; no evidence supports reverting them.
- **Roof/perimeter joins:** smooth visible stair-step silhouette artifacts at clipped roof/lightwell boundaries while retaining current traced positions. Cleaning joins is supported; inventing new aperture dimensions is not.
- **One documented write-up module:** improve service uprights, shelf thickness, mobile cabinet/handle/caster silhouette and task-chair construction using the existing manufacturer evidence. Propagate a small number of plausible variants only where these systems are photographed. Avoid a huge inventory of invented equipment.
- **Reference registration:** allow one bounded refinement where a clear landmark pair warrants it. Keep all twelve comparison cameras as evidence; make new cinematic cameras separately. The known hero rail elbow problem is not solved by a broad southwest sweep, as prior tests demonstrated.

Exact roof-aperture dimensions, hidden stair fabrication and landing angles, full office-pod topology, the unidentified complete mural, exact furniture product attribution, room numbering outside verified photographs, and measured exterior-building placement. Preserve these as documented approximations. The partial exterior facade must not become a featured view. Do not replace missing context with invented detailed streetscape and then label it accurate.
