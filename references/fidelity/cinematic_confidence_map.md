# ISEC architectural confidence for the finite cinematic pass

Prepared 2026-09-07 from existing primary references, published plans, and the completed research notes. No scene edits or new dimensional discoveries. Current implementation status is taken from `current_pass_report.md` and `camera_discrepancies.md` (post60); this document does not independently certify the latest Blender/Unreal scene or export.

**HIGH** means directly established by a published drawing or multiple built photographs for the specific claim. **MEDIUM** means a supported approximation, photo-fit, design-stage dimension, or bounded reconstruction. **LOW** means unresolved exact geometry, hidden construction, sparse evidence, or an inferred identity/placement. A HIGH relationship does not make its numeric dimensions or material shader HIGH. Cinematic suitability describes architectural credibility, not Unreal performance or collision validation.

## Recommended finite cinematic scope

Use a small sequence of public-interior views: establishing atrium; lower-stair approach; a restrained upper-stair elevation/orbit; social hub; laboratory frontage; and an interior-facing end lounge. The twelve reference cameras are composition anchors, not calibrated camera tracks. Connect positions only after checking the actual exported scene's floor support, guard/stair clearance, clipping, glass behavior and streaming/material appearance. This research does not establish that Unreal validation has passed.

Roof, balcony and exterior uncertainties do not justify an open-ended modeling phase. Maintain their documented broad relationships and preserve uncertainty labels. Treat this finite pass as polish and cinematic presentation of a reference-guided reconstruction; the prior report explicitly says the user's photographic-match definition of done has not yet been met. A cinematic render alone does not change that assessment.
