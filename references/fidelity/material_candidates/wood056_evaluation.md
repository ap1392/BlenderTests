# Photometric wood candidate

Candidate source: [ambientCG Wood 056](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood056).
The publisher identifies photometric stereo, approximately 40 × 40 cm coverage,
and CC0 licensing. Downloaded the 1K JPEG PBR set for a bounded material test.
The images are an independent wood sample, not a scan of Northeastern ISEC; the
publisher does not identify the species as white oak.

Potential use: subtle pore/normal and roughness variation in the photographed
pale wood furniture. Current procedural material has conspicuous repeated linear
bands in REF04. The candidate's orange color and surface scratches must not be
copied indiscriminately: only a conservative, reference-calibrated pale tone and
restrained relief are appropriate. The master should change only after an actual
render comparison demonstrates an improvement. This download alone is not an
accepted material replacement. Do not load or execute the bundled Blender file;
use the image maps only.

## Render decision

Rejected after the isolated REF04 trial (`REF04_Writeup_photometric61.png`).
The pale remapping suppressed the directional grain and made the end panel read
as beige laminate. Both the parent and independent visual audit judged the
existing material closer to the photographed oak, while recognizing its overly
regular grain. The trial never changed or saved the live master. Its downloaded
image maps remain an unused candidate, not accepted project materials.
