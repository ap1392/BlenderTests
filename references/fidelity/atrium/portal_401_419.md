## Photo attribution — corrected after sightline check

**Withdraw the earlier eastern attribution.** It considered horizontal projection but omitted the door-facing direction and occlusion. From current REF07 camera (+16.2,+12.3), the eastern door is seen from its corridor/back side. A ray to its midpoint (-0.9,-12.05) crosses approximately (0.89,-9.5), inside the kitchenette/office-bank footprint north of the door. The visible front portal in Wausau4 cannot be justified at this eastern location from that viewpoint.

The **WESTERN opening is the viable candidate**. Its plan segment (-9.1,-8.8) to (-6.9,-10.0) has a northeast-facing normal approximately (0.48,0.88), oriented toward the REF07 camera. The ray approaches its exposed hub side without passing through the kitchenette. The closed door pair and surround belong at that cross-corridor throat between the curved meeting-room pod and the rectangular office to its south, not across the kitchenette or the whole social hub.

Wausau4 shows only a narrow portion of this wall and no complete adjoining plan topology. Thus the western location is **moderate-confidence correspondence**, not a verified 401–419 room-number mapping. The plan independently supports the actual double-door geometry at that segment; the photograph supports its green/gray appearance. A 401–419 label at that location should remain explicitly inferred until a second view or numbered plan confirms it.

For implementation: retain the full approximately 2.5 m plan opening as a closed pair (a photograph may show only one leaf due to perspective/occlusion). Use a wall/portal plane normal toward NE. The built photo supports a dark green closed surface, a yellow-green reveal, gray/white surround and an overhead label; exact leaf division and header dimensions are unresolved. Do not use the prior eastern location for the photographed portal.
