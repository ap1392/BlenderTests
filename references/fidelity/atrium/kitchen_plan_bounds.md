The plan's oval island is a separate outline without a connection to the back counter. The built photo instead shows the curved bar/peninsula treatment, so the plan proves the location and approximate footprint of the central work/bar element more strongly than its precise as-built form. Relative to the supplied current peninsula bounds x[-.43,.41], y[-10.3,-7.8], the plan oval is centered roughly **0.44 m farther west and 1.0 m farther north**, and is about 0.45 m wider. Enlarging it to cover the documented counter plus island footprint is a photo-informed modeling inference, not a traced ceiling plan. ## Island topology

The oval is one complete closed outline. There is no C/U opening, no connection to the rear counter, no internal dashed return, and no staff-side gap in that outline. Use a closed freestanding rounded body; the staff aisle is the open space between the island and the sink/back counter. Photo03's white rounded end, lounge-side stools and person behind the bar are consistent with this arrangement. A U-shaped body would be an unsupported addition. The exact tapered capsule contour may be approximated from the closed plan outline, whose measured maximum width is about 1.3 m and length about 2.4 m.
