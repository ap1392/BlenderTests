# Independent architecture review: refined A

Inspected REF01, REF09, REF10 and REF12 refined A PNGs against their source photographs. Also read scripts 03, 04, 07, 08, 12, 13 and 14. Cleanup 14 occurred after these renders: duplicate end guards, slab unions, floating ground drums, beam pegs and cushion refinements are therefore **excluded from the outstanding recommendations** below. Those fixes require a new render for verification.

## REF01 — Payette atrium

1. **Far end bridge ceilings are incomplete.** Render shows bright white gaps/soffits and floating-looking drum silhouettes across the far galleries. The reference has continuous wood ceiling fields with dark backing, punctuated by luminous drums. Script 04 clips blades to the curved south-gallery footprint and local hubs; the corrected end-bridge footprints created later by script 08 need equivalent ceiling geometry. Add backing and clipped straight blades over the full bridge lounge footprints, including the junction with the gallery.
2. **Glazed laboratory depth still reads too empty.** The new green fields and benches are progress, but the near write-up strip is mostly identical desks/monitors. Source has denser shelving, divider panels and chairs, with strong white structural frames behind the second glass. Complete a few accurate repeating lab modules rather than more single monitors. Keep depth visible through clear zones without inventing accessible interiors.
3. **Concrete reads as masonry tile.** The enclosure uses a highly regular staggered brick pattern. Reference board-formed concrete has long horizontal timber impressions with irregular end joints and subtle streaking. Replace the visible brick-color pattern with broad, low-contrast board variation and finer directional grain; keep joints sparse and imperfect. Current geometry still needs the stair agent's contour comparison.
4. **The ceiling-to-glass junction is too sharp and diagrammatic.** Source has dark recessed perimeter structure, slender framing and a coherent ceiling edge. Render directly joins dense glass mullions to a bright ceiling edge. Add/verify a narrow dark perimeter reveal and consistent header depth; do not thicken the main glass bars.
5. **Photographic composition still differs.** The lower stair foot is close to a useful landmark, but the spiral's broad ribbons occupy too much of the left-middle image relative to the source. Preserve the calibrated near/far landmarks and adjust yaw/lens using at least one upper landing point before changing stair diameter. This residual may be camera-dependent rather than a geometry error.

## REF09 — Ground wide

1. **Public frontage remains a blank wall/wedge at left.** Cleanup slab union may improve seams but does not add the real recessed doorway/elevator/classroom composition. Use source 011 and the verified ground plan to add full-height recesses, dark headers and the correct closed doors within this region. It should not read as one uninterrupted white shelf over an empty hall.
2. **The far bridge ceiling issue repeats strongly here.** The missing dark/wood fields remove depth and make the upper levels look like outdoor balconies. Add the actual overhead enclosure over the bridge footprint before adjusting exposure.
3. **Floor bands have an anomalous dark ridge.** The foreground right inlay appears as a raised or folded seam rather than a flush tonal change. Script 07 nominally places the inlay only about 4 mm above the floor, so check mesh normals/self-intersections and grazing reflections before changing height. Prefer one floor surface with material regions or a flush decal-like overlay, avoiding overlapping extruded boundaries.
4. **Furnishing groups are still unlike the reference.** The source foreground includes several distinct tables and thin white chairs, with wire-base lounge seating on the right and magenta seating behind. Render groups are sparse and concentrated differently. Once the revised camera is accepted, place the large groups using the photograph's floor contacts; do not compensate by scaling chairs globally.
5. **Ceiling blades lack the black service layer.** The right public ceiling is now in frame, but its appearance is an even brown sheet with parallel lines. Source reveals black cross-carriers, dark gaps and varying shadow depth between blades. Verify backing placement above the blades, add a restrained carrier rhythm and keep the light fixture housings recessed into this field. This is separate from cleanup 14's lamp relocation.

## REF10 — Near-frontal laboratory glass

1. **Possible frit placement defect.** `panel_glass` makes a 12 mm ribbon. Script 03 places frit at offset 11 mm with 1 mm thickness, exactly ending on the glass face. That risks coplanar or embedded surfaces. Move frit 1–2 mm outside the glass face and test a high-resolution crop. Do not simply thicken all stripes before testing: 2.2 mm lines at 850 px preview width are already subpixel, so preview resolution also limits visibility.
2. **Write-up and laboratory zones still merge visually.** Add a clearly legible second glazing line with its frames, blue/gray desk dividers and chairs in front, and white spine shelves behind. Current desks line up with lab benches and suppress the intended separation. Small lateral offsets between modules will create real depth cues.
3. **Laboratory services remain too sparse.** The reference contains multiple white vertical shelf uprights, cabinet blocks, a few suspended service loops and varied equipment silhouettes. The new long benches are useful, but render still resembles an empty computer classroom. Add shelf uprights and limited reference-visible equipment to representative bays; avoid arbitrary clutter.
4. **Ceiling rafts need dark separation.** Reference suspended white panels are bounded by dark structure and several slim pendant fixtures. Render's large uninterrupted white ceiling areas should be broken into separate rafts with a dark layer above. Ensure lights illuminate the raft undersides/adjacent surfaces instead of producing isolated blown-out rectangles.
5. **Spandrel saturation/contrast.** Blue bands currently dominate more than in the photograph, while the clear zones are pale. First fix the interior/reflection hierarchy; then slightly reduce band saturation if still necessary. Preserve the now plausible approximately 31% band-height ratio. Do not return to the original shallow bands.

## REF12 — Elevated roof reverse

1. **Alcove massing is still too rectangular.** The murals and counters are visible, but each hub reads as a repeated white box. Reference has curved enclosing volumes and deep asymmetric openings behind the stair. Add rounded side returns and the curved upper soffit around the actual kitchenette opening, based on plan and photos. A mural is a detail, not a replacement for the enclosure silhouette.
2. **Three skylights are not yet framed.** The view shows two while the source shows three. Aim modestly toward the lab side to shift the stair right and adjust camera position/pitch to admit the near opening. Only after all three are visible should hole centers be corrected from this camera.
3. **Missing skylight glazing grid.** The upper aperture is a featureless blue/white panel. Reflections 024 clearly shows several slender rectilinear bars crossing the glazing. Add a clipped grid of roughly three longitudinal bars and two crossbars as an initial photo-informed pattern, then fit count/spacing to the close-up. Keep the reveal smooth and the bars at the actual upper plane, not on the lower mouth.
4. **Ceiling field continuity.** Repeated exposed blade ends and bright gaps around the galleries undermine the continuous roof enclosure. Apply the full bridge/hub ceiling footprint correction and add a clean perimeter termination. Cleanup 14 unions slabs but does not, by itself, extend the wood ceiling fields.
5. **Lighting is too uniformly cool and flat.** Real photo combines warm interior pockets, darker overhead cavities, strong glass reflections and bright skylights. Render has pale laboratory bays and similarly bright white office walls throughout. After completing the enclosure, reduce uniform fill and use the real local fixtures to establish warmth and depth; preserve daylight from the apertures.

## Highest-return next steps

1. Complete ceiling fields over the corrected bridge footprints and their dark backing.
2. Fix frit placement and check it at higher resolution.
3. Add skylight glazing bars and refine the three-aperture camera framing.
4. Shape the actual alcove enclosure and ground-frontage recesses.
5. Replace masonry-like concrete color pattern and investigate the floor-inlay ridge.

These findings are additional to cleanup 14. The renders show continued improvement, but the room enclosure and visible depth remain the strongest distinctions from real photography.
