ISEC reconstruction reference register — collected 2026-09-06

01 PAYETTE — built project, primary architectural source
https://www.payette.com/project/northeasternisec/
Downloaded project HTML and published Level 1, 2, 4 plans; ten professional photos.
Establishes six-storey central atrium, curved office wing, north glazed lab wing,
end bridges, ground communal seating, circulation connections and three roof oculi.
Plans are traced in XY; no readable scale bar or dimension strings in these images.
Photo credits on source: Keitaro Yoshioka; Ngoc X Doan; Warren Jagger.
Images retained locally for reference, not used as browser textures.

02 PAYETTE — Spiral Stair, 13 August 2015
https://www.payette.com/projects/spiral-stair-northeastern-university-isec/
Built upper stair circular in plan; earlier elliptical version explicitly rejected.
Fabrication in three segments per full spiral; floor landing connections.
Article HTML saved. Original archive shop-drawing image URLs returned network
errors; shop drawings have NOT been read and are not dimension evidence.

03 Summit Engineering — 2017 SENH award
https://www.summitengineeringinc.com/summit-engineering-receives-2017-senh-excellence-in-structural-engineering-award/
Primary structural account. Inner/outer plate stringers, bent plate treads,
intermediate trapezoidal landings, A-frame floor supports, tubular tread framing.
Lowest flight differs from repeated upper flights. Radial glass guards.
Read via web tool; reference-image download unavailable.

04 PAYETTE — Below Grade, 29 April 2014
https://www.payette.com/projects/northeastern-university-isec-below-grade/
Columbus entrance at L1; pedestrian bridge entrance at L2; wide connecting stair;
auditorium partly embedded in grade. Used for circulation hierarchy, not details
of unbuilt design images.

05 PAYETTE — Interiors Update, 29 September 2016
https://www.payette.com/?p=7219
Grey/white layered interior, charcoal carpet, bright social hubs, blue/green
furniture. Photograph slide-6-7 used to establish gallery seating and kitchenette.

06 PAYETTE — From Oocyte to City and Back
